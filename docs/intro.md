# Leaving School Wiki

This wiki is a reference companion to the Leaving School course. Every concept, tool, and framework covered in the programme has an entry here.

## How to use it

- **Search** — use the search bar (top right) to look up any term or topic
- **Browse** — navigate via the sidebar to explore topics by category
- **Links** — entries link to related concepts, so you can follow threads

## What's in here

Each entry covers:
- What the concept is and why it matters
- How it applies to the course material
- Any practical tips or watch-outs

## Contributing

Entries are maintained by the Leaving School team. If you spot an error or gap, flag it to the team.
